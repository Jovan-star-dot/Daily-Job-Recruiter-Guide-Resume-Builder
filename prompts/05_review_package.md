# Review an application package

Provide the master resume, proof points, profile, posting, and generated documents.

```text
Review this application package against my master resume, confirmed proof points, candidate profile, and the actual job posting.

Check every added or rewritten factual claim, job/degree dates, metrics and their employers, tool proficiency, academic versus professional experience, eligibility, requirement coverage, and supported ATS keywords. Check score arithmetic and threshold if a match score was provided. Reverify the live official application link when possible; state what you cannot verify.

Render and inspect the documents when tools are available. Check the requested page length, readable text, spacing, overflow, contact details, and correct company/role. If you cannot inspect the layout, explicitly leave that check unresolved. Compare selectable document text as well as appearance.

Give specific corrections and produce corrected documents when possible. Do not retain unsupported claims. Assign APPROVED, NEEDS YOUR REVIEW, or REJECT JOB, with reasons and a concise list of completed/uncompleted checks. APPROVED still requires my final review. Do not contact anyone or submit an application.
```
